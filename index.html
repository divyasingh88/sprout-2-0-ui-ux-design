Good to know

Questions, without the fine print.

What should I have ready?

Keep your PAN and Aadhaar handy for identity verification. The app will guide you through the information it needs.

How much can I start with?

You can explore an initial investment from ₹100. The amount is your choice; investing involves market risk and returns are not assured.

What if verification takes a while?

You’ll see a clear in-progress message while verification is underway. You don’t need to keep tapping or restart the process.
